Add fast-match support for rapid 1:1 call switching. `Room.keepLocalMediaOnDisconnect` keeps local tracks (including the default camera/mic tracks), their capture pipelines, and the audio session alive across disconnects so back-to-back calls never reopen the camera or rebuild audio routing; retained media is released by `Room.release()`. `Room.switchRoom(url, token)` switches a connected room to a different room through a new `SWITCHING` state — the signal connection and peer connections are rebuilt for the new room while the same local track objects are republished, with no `DISCONNECTED` state or `Disconnected` event in between; concurrent switches abort-and-replace safely (`RoomEvent.RoomSwitching`/`RoomSwitched`). A new `FastMatchSession` façade (`io.livekit.android.fastmatch`) wraps the whole flow — warm-up (`start`, `prepareNext`), match lifecycle events, in-match chat, and per-match latency metrics including time-to-first-frame. Connect speedups: audio and video now publish concurrently on connect (`Participant.addTrackPublication` is synchronized accordingly), and the remote-media-before-metadata retry uses progressive backoff (10ms first retry instead of 150ms). Full-reconnect republishing is hardened against cancellation: parked publications merge across repeated attempts and an interrupted republish re-parks its remainder instead of losing tracks.
